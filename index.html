Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Somda's KYC and identity verification services

How long does the verification process take?

Our verification process typically completes in less than 5 seconds for most clients. The speed depends on several factors including the quality of the submitted documents, network connectivity, and the complexity of the client's profile. For clients with complex backgrounds or multiple jurisdictions, the process may take a few minutes as our system conducts more thorough checks. We've optimized every step of our pipeline to ensure rapid verification without compromising on accuracy or compliance rigor.

What documents are required for verification?

The required documents depend on your specific compliance requirements and the client's jurisdiction. At minimum, we typically require:

  • A valid government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, national ID)
  • Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, or government document)
  • A selfie or live photo for biometric matching

For higher-risk clients or enhanced due diligence requirements, additional documentation such as source of funds verification or business registration documents may be required. Our system is flexible and can be configured to match your specific compliance framework.

Is Somda compliant with international regulations?

Yes, Somda is designed to comply with the world's most stringent regulatory frameworks. Our platform supports compliance with:

  • FATF Recommendations - The Financial Action Task Force standards for AML/CFT
  • EU GDPR & eIDAS - European data protection and digital identity regulations
  • US FinCEN & OFAC - US sanctions and AML requirements
  • Local Regulations - 180+ country-specific compliance requirements

Our compliance team continuously monitors regulatory changes and updates our system accordingly. We maintain comprehensive documentation for audit purposes and work closely with our clients to ensure their specific regulatory requirements are met.

How do you protect customer data and privacy?

Data security and privacy are paramount at Somda. We employ multiple layers of protection:

  • End-to-End Encryption - All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256 encryption
  • ISO 27001 Certification - Our infrastructure meets international information security standards
  • GDPR Compliance - We respect user privacy rights and data minimization principles
  • Regular Audits - Third-party security audits and penetration testing

Additionally, biometric data is processed securely and never stored in its raw form. We use advanced anonymization techniques and maintain strict access controls to ensure that only authorized personnel can access sensitive information.

What is the pricing model and how do we get started?

Somda offers flexible pricing models designed to scale with your business:

  • Pay-Per-Verification - You only pay for each verification performed, with volume discounts available
  • Enterprise Plans - Custom pricing for high-volume users with dedicated support
  • Free Trial - Start with 100 free verifications to test our platform

To get started, visit our contact page or email info@somda.co.za. Our onboarding team will guide you through the setup process, which typically takes less than 24 hours. We'll help you configure the system to match your specific compliance requirements and provide training for your team.
